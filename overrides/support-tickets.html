Quick help articles

How do I report a problem?

Enter a clear subject, select a category and priority, and describe what happened. Send one ticket and keep its ID for reference.

Where can I track my request?

Open Support Tickets and switch between Open and Resolved. The Launch Era updates the status while reviewing your request.

What details should I include?

Describe the feature, what you were trying to do, what appeared instead, and your device. Never include passwords or a customer's private details.

Where can I find my bookings?

Return to your Command Center and choose Booking Calendar. If bookings are missing or not updating, select Bookings when creating a ticket.
